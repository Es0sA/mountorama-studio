PG. 04 · QUESTIONS

Before you ask

How much does a comic cost?

Standard pricing is $100 per page. Promotional pricing ($70–80/page) runs periodically. The banner at the top of this page shows if one's active, and when it ends.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit is due after you receive a quote. Upload your payment receipt, and production begins once it's confirmed. The remaining 50% is due before final files are delivered.

Do you print physical copies?

No, Mountorama delivers digital files only (PDF and JPG by default). Printing is handled on your end.
