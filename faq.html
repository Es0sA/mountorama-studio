Questions,
answered.

Starter set below. If yours isn't here, ask directly once you're in the request form or your dashboard.

What comic styles do you offer?

American, Chinese, and Korean styles, each handled by a specific artist on the Mountorama team. Your style choice determines who draws your pages.

Do you print physical copies?

No, Mountorama delivers digital files only. Printing is handled by you.

How much does a comic cost?

Standard pricing is $100 per page. Promotional pricing ($70–80/page) runs periodically. Check the homepage for active promotions and countdowns.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit is due after you receive a quote. Upload your payment receipt for confirmation before work begins. The remaining 50% balance is due before final files are delivered.

What file formats do I get?

PDF and JPG by default. PSD files are available as a separate paid add-on.

How many revisions do I get?

2 rounds for line art and 2 rounds for colored pages, by default. Additional rounds can be arranged directly with the studio.

Can I order multiple comics at once?

Yes, bundle up to 5 comic books in a single request, with a 50% deposit due on each.

What currency do you charge in?

Pricing is set per client based on your currency; it isn't fixed to Naira.

Can I message the studio directly?

Yes, each order includes a chat section for direct communication with the studio and your assigned artist.

Still have a question?

Start a request and ask directly. The studio replies with your quote.
